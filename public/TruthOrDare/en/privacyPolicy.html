Truth or Dare

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

In short

Truth or Dare (“App”; Polish name “Prawda czy Wyzwanie”) is made by Dawikk (“we”, “us”). The App has no user accounts and no server of ours, and it uses no analytics tools. Player names, your own cards, settings and game history are stored only on your phone.

Data leaves your phone in three situations only: when ads are shown (Google AdMob – not with Premium), when you buy or restore Premium (the store and RevenueCat), and during multiplayer on several phones, when phones on the same Wi‑Fi network exchange the game state with each other.

1. Data stored on your device

The data below is kept in the App's private storage. We do not send it to ourselves or anyone else.

DataPurpose
PlayersNames or nicknames entered before a game, your name for multiplayer and a list of recent players, so you don't have to type them every time.
GameThe state of the current game (order, round, points, passes, jokers, drawn cards) so it can be resumed.
Game historyDate, mode, decks, duration and the names and scores of players from the last 100 finished games. The App uses them to calculate stats and player records.
My cardsQuestions and dares you write yourself.
Reported cardsIDs of cards you flagged, so they don't show up again. Reports are not sent anywhere.
SettingsLanguage, haptics, game mode and rules, and the remembered Premium status, so Premium works offline too.

You don't need to enter your real name or any contact details. A nickname is perfectly fine.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). Google's ads SDK may collect and process, among other things: the device advertising ID (Android Advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and operating system information, App information and data about ad impressions and interactions. Google uses this data to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and – if you agree – personalize ads.

  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the App shows Google's consent form (UMP) on first launch. Personalized ads are shown only with your consent. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy.
  • Family mode. In Family mode, ad requests are tagged as child-directed and for users under the age of consent, ads are non-personalized only, and the ad content rating is limited to content suitable for everyone (G).
  • Premium. With Premium active, the App does not start ads and sends no ad requests.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google → Ads).

More: how Google uses data from partner apps, Google Privacy Policy.

3. Premium purchase

Premium is a one-time purchase on Google Play (or the App Store). Payment is handled by the store – we have no access to your card or other payment details.

The purchase status is verified and stored by RevenueCat, Inc. (USA), acting as our processor. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous ID assigned to the App (not your name, email or advertising ID);
  • purchase information: product, price and currency, date and the purchase receipt issued by the store;
  • basic technical data: operating system and version, App version, store country and the connection's IP address.

This data is used only to grant and restore Premium (also on another device with the same store account) and for aggregated sales statistics. RevenueCat does not receive player names, cards or game history. Transfers to the USA are based on standard contractual clauses. RevenueCat's policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

4. Multiplayer on several phones (Wi‑Fi)

“Play on several phones” works only on your local network, without the internet and without any server of ours. Phones connect to each other directly.

  • Room announcement. The host's phone announces the room on the local network (mDNS / Bonjour). The announcement contains the host's name, the game mode, the protocol version and the address and port – other devices on the same network can see it. The room code is not announced: only the people you show it to (e.g. with the QR code) know it.
  • What is sent. Phones in a room exchange: player names, the game state (order, cards, points, passes, jokers, votes) and emoji reactions. The host's phone saves the game just like a single-phone game (section 1); guests' phones don't save its history.
  • When the room is closed, connections are dropped and the announcement disappears from the network.

Use this mode on a trusted network (e.g. at home). On public Wi‑Fi, anyone on the same network may see the host's name.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't run accounts and have no server with your data.
  • We don't use analytics tools or Firebase.
  • We don't sell data. Data reaches third parties only as described in sections 2 and 3.
  • We never send your own cards, reported cards or game history anywhere.

7. Retention and deletion

  • You keep the App's data for as long as you like. Clear the history on the History screen and delete your own cards in My cards. Uninstalling the App removes all its data from the phone.
  • Ad-related data is retained by Google under its own policies.
  • RevenueCat keeps purchase data for as long as needed to handle the purchase and as required by tax law; on request we delete the anonymous ID associated with the App.
  • If system backup to Google or iCloud is turned on, a copy of the App's data may be stored in your account – this is a system feature controlled in your phone settings.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. Family mode is meant for families playing together under adult supervision; in this mode ads are non-personalized and tagged as child-directed (section 2). If you believe a child has provided us with data, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Security

The App's data is kept in its private directory, protected by the operating system and device encryption. Connections to Google and RevenueCat are encrypted. A local game room can only be joined with its 4‑digit code, and the host can remove any guest before the game starts.

10. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data and to object to processing, and you may withdraw consent to personalized ads at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Poland: the President of the Personal Data Protection Office, UODO). Data stored in the App is fully under your control on the phone; for purchase data, contact us.

11. Changes to this policy

We announce changes by publishing a new version on this page with a new effective date. If the App were to start sending data in a way other than described above, we will describe it here before such a version reaches the store.
