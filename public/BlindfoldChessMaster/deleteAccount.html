Delete Account

Effective date: 2025-01-01

This page explains how to delete your data for the Blindfold Chess Trainer application ("the App") by Dawid Wrzesiński ("Dawikk").

Delete your account & data

In the app: open Settings → Account → Delete account and confirm. This permanently removes your account and the data associated with it.

Prefer email? Send a request from your account's email address to dawikk.apps@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". We will process verified requests within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account identifier and profile information.
  • App data linked to your account (such as progress, settings and saved content).

Some records may be retained where required by law or for fraud prevention, for the minimum period necessary.

Contact

Need help? Email us at dawikk.apps@gmail.com.
