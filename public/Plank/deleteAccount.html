Delete Account

Effective date: 2025-01-01

This page explains how to delete your data for the Plank Challenge application ("the App") by Dawid Wrzesiński ("Dawikk").

Your data is stored on your device

Plank Challenge does not require an account. Your settings, progress and other data are stored locally on your device. To delete all of it, simply uninstall the App — this removes the associated data from your device.

If we hold any data relating to you (for example, diagnostics) and you would like it deleted, email a request to dawikk.apps@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.
