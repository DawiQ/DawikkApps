1. The service

Restie helps parents and caregivers log a baby's or young child's sleep, feeds and nappies, see the day at a glance, get age-based guidance and, after a few days of entries, forecasts of the next nap and bedtime. Optional Family & sync lets up to 6 caregivers share one log. Logging, history, every child profile, night mode, the age-based nap window, the 14-day PDF report, CSV export, backup and family sync are free. Some features require Restie Unlimited (section 5).

2. Not medical advice

Restie is not a medical device

Forecasts, nap windows, sleep norms, regression and nap-transition hints, and the sleep guide are general information based on typical patterns and the entries you make. They are not a diagnosis or treatment and do not replace a paediatrician, midwife or other health professional. Every child is different. If you are worried about your child's sleep, feeding, breathing, growth or health, contact a doctor. In an emergency call your local emergency number. Always follow safe-sleep recommendations from health authorities.

Forecasts depend on the accuracy and completeness of your entries and may be wrong. Reminders depend on your phone's notification settings and may arrive late or not at all.
