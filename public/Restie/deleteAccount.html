Delete your account

Last updated: 12 October 2026

An account in Restie exists only if you turned on Family & sync and signed in with Apple or Google. If you never did, there is no account and nothing about you is stored on our servers; uninstalling the App removes all its data from your phone.

Delete it in the App

  1. Open Restie and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Family & sync.
  3. In the Account section tap Delete account.
  4. Choose whether to keep your entries on this phone or clear them too, then sign in with Apple or Google again to confirm.

If you own a family that has other members, first make someone else the owner (Family & sync → Members), or remove the others and delete the family.

Can't use the App?

Email dawikk.apps@gmail.com with the subject “Restie – delete my account”, from the email address of the Apple or Google account you signed in with (if you used Apple's “Hide My Email”, mention that and add your RevenueCat customer ID from Settings → Subscription if you can). We will confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (Firebase Authentication) and your user record, including your consent record.
  • Your family membership. If you were the only member, the whole family is deleted with all its children, entries, tasks and notes.
  • Registered phones and push notification tokens.
  • Your Restie Unlimited status stored on our server, and join-attempt counters.
  • On request, your purchase history in RevenueCat.

Deletion from our servers is immediate when done in the App and cannot be undone.

What is kept

  • Entries in a family you leave: if other caregivers stay in the family, the children and entries remain available to them, since they are part of the shared log. Entries you created are no longer linked to an account.
  • Data on your phone, unless you choose to clear it. Backup, CSV and PDF files you exported stay wherever you saved them.
  • Purchase records held by Apple, Google and RevenueCat for billing, refunds and tax law.
  • Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports (only if you allowed them) are not linked to your account and expire on their own (crash reports after 90 days, statistics within 14 months).

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription

Restie Unlimited is billed by the App Store or Google Play. Cancel it in your store account (or in Restie: Settings → Subscription → Manage subscription) before deleting your account. If you were paying for your family, the other members lose Unlimited when you delete your account.
