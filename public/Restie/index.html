Help & FAQ

Answers to the most common questions.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store. On Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Restie. On iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Restie. In Restie you can go there from Settings → Subscription → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends; you keep Unlimited until then. Uninstalling the app does not cancel it.

I bought Unlimited but it is not active

Open Settings → Subscription → Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it. If a payment is waiting for approval (for example a family payment request), Unlimited turns on as soon as the store confirms it; do not buy again. Still stuck? Email us with your customer ID from Settings → Subscription.

How does the free trial work?

The yearly plan may start with a 7-day free trial, one per store account. The price and the date of the first charge are shown before you start. If you allowed notifications, Restie reminds you 2 days before the trial ends. Cancel before then and you will not be charged.

Does one purchase cover my partner too?

Yes. When you are signed in and in a Restie family, Unlimited bought by any member covers everyone in that family (up to 6 people), on Android and iPhone. Only the person who bought it can manage or cancel it.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store: Google Play refunds or Apple – report a problem.

How do I redeem a promo code?

Go to Settings → Subscription → Redeem code.

How do I invite another caregiver?

Go to Settings → Family & sync, sign in with Apple or Google and create a family, then tap Invite a caregiver. The other person installs Restie, taps I have a family invite and enters the code or scans the QR code. Invites are valid for 24 hours.

I have a new phone. How do I move my data?

With Family & sync, just sign in on the new phone. Without it, create a backup in Settings → Backups & export on the old phone and restore it on the new one.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. Only if you turn on Family & sync is it also stored on Google Cloud servers in Warsaw, Poland. Read the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Family & sync → Delete account. Details on the Delete account page.

Is Restie medical advice?

No. Forecasts and guidance are general information and do not replace a paediatrician. If you are worried about your child's health, contact a doctor.
