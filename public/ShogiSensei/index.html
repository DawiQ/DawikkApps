Help & FAQ

Answers to the most common questions.

I have never played Shogi. Where do I start?

Open the Learn tab and start chapter 1. Each lesson is a few minutes long and you make the moves yourself. After the first chapters, try the AI on level 1 or 2. The Today tab always shows the next lesson and the day's puzzles.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. The lessons, the AI, the puzzles and your game library all work without internet. An internet connection is used only for ads, purchases, the optional sign-in and app updates.

Which AI level should I choose?

Level 1 suits beginners who have just learned the rules. Each level gives the engine more time to think. Progress shows your win rate on each level; when you win most games, move up.

Can I switch to Western piece letters or notation?

Yes. In Settings → Board choose kanji or Western letters for the pieces, and Japanese, letter or number square notation. Coordinates on the board frame can be turned on or off.

Which languages are available?

English, Japanese and Polish, for both the app and the lessons. By default the app follows your device language; change it in Settings → Language.

How do the daily streak and freezes work?

Open at least one of the day's puzzles to add a day to your streak. If you miss a day, a freeze covers it. You get one freeze a week and can hold two.

How do I import or export a game?

Open the Library. Any saved game can be shared as KIF text (with a machine-readable USI line) or as an image card. Use Import game to paste a KIF, USI or SFEN.

I bought Unlimited but I still see ads

Go to Settings → Unlimited → Restore purchases while signed in to the same Google or Apple account you bought it with. If you signed in to ShogiSensei when you bought it, sign in with the same Apple or Google account again. Still stuck? Email us with the App User ID from Settings → Unlimited → Purchases & receipts.

I have a new phone. How do I keep Unlimited?

On the same store (Android to Android, iPhone to iPhone), tap Restore purchases. To move between iPhone and Android, sign in with Google (or Apple) in Settings → Account on the old phone, then sign in with the same account on the new one. Lesson progress and saved games stay on the phone they were made on; export the games you want to keep as KIF.

How do I redeem a promo code?

Go to Settings → Unlimited → Redeem a code. On iPhone the App Store's code sheet opens; on Android Google Play opens with your code.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store: Google Play refunds or Apple – report a problem.

How do I change my ad and privacy choices?

Go to Settings → Privacy choices. Declining personalised ads does not limit anything in the app. Read the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account or my data?

An account is optional. If you signed in: Settings → Account → Delete account. Lesson progress can be cleared in Settings → Reset progress, and uninstalling removes everything from the phone. Details on the Delete account page.
