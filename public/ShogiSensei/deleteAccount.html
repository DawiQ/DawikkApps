Delete your account

Last updated: 13 October 2026

An account in ShogiSensei is optional and exists only if you signed in with Apple or Google in Settings → Account. Its only job is to carry Unlimited to other phones. ShogiSensei has no server of its own that stores your account, progress or games.

Delete it in the App

  1. Open ShogiSensei and go to Settings.
  2. In the Account section tap Delete account…
  3. Confirm with Delete account.
  4. Signed in with Apple: Apple does not let an app revoke Sign in with Apple by itself. To finish, open Settings → [your name] → Sign in with Apple → ShogiSensei → Stop Using on your iPhone.

With Google, access is revoked from the App immediately. You can also remove it yourself in your Google account under Third-party connections.

Delete your purchase data

Your purchase record in RevenueCat (the service that validates Unlimited) is not removed by the steps above, so that Restore purchases keeps working. To have it deleted, email dawikk.apps@gmail.com with the subject “ShogiSensei – delete my data” and your App User ID from Settings → Unlimited → Purchases & receipts. We confirm the request and delete it within 30 days. You can also use this address if you can no longer open the App.

Delete the data on your phone

  • Lesson progress, review schedule and streak: Settings → Reset progress.
  • Saved games: open a game in the Library and tap Delete.
  • Everything: uninstall the App.
  • Ad and analytics consent: change it in Settings → Privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

What is deleted

  • The link between the App and your Apple or Google account, and the name and email stored on your phone.
  • Google's sign-in access for ShogiSensei (Apple's after you tap Stop Using).
  • On request, your purchase history and App User ID in RevenueCat.

What is kept

  • Progress and games on your phone, until you reset or delete them as above.
  • Purchase records held by Apple and Google, and those RevenueCat must keep for billing, refunds and tax law.
  • Usage statistics (only if you allowed them) are not linked to your account and expire within 14 months.

Deleting your account does not remove Unlimited

Unlimited stays with your App Store or Google Play account. Settings → Unlimited → Restore purchases brings it back on the same store. It is a one-time purchase, so there is nothing to cancel.
