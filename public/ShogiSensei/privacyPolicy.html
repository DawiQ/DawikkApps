Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

In short

  • ShogiSensei works fully without an account. Your lessons, games, puzzles, streak and settings are stored only on your phone. We have no server that stores them.
  • The free version shows ads by Google AdMob and its mediation partners. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you choose whether ads may be personalised; on iPhone the system asks whether apps may track you. Saying no changes nothing in the app.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase counts how the app is used (for example how far people get in the course), following the same consent. It never receives your games or your name.
  • Unlimited is bought through Google Play or the App Store and validated by RevenueCat. We never see your card details.
  • Signing in with Apple or Google is optional and only carries Unlimited to another phone. You can delete it in Settings → Account.
  • We do not sell personal data.

1. Who we are

ShogiSensei (the “App”) is an app for learning and playing Shogi (Japanese chess), developed by Dawikk Apps (“we”, “us”), who is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact for anything about privacy: dawikk.apps@gmail.com.

2. Data on your phone

The App, including its Shogi engine, runs on your device. What you do in it is saved in the App's private storage and never sent to us.

3. Ads and consent

The free version shows a banner, an interstitial ad after some games, an occasional offer to support the App when you return to it, and rewarded ads you choose to watch (for an extra hint or a board skin). Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may fill them through mediation partners: AppLovin, Chartboost, InMobi, ironSource, Liftoff (Vungle), Meta Audience Network, Pangle and Unity Ads.

To show and measure ads, and to prevent ad fraud, these companies may collect:

  • the advertising ID of your device (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, the latter only if you allow tracking), and the app set ID;
  • the IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • device and OS model, language, network type, the App's name and version;
  • interactions with ads: impressions, clicks, views of rewarded ads.

Your choice. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the App shows Google's consent form (a certified consent management platform under the IAB TCF) before ads are requested. On iPhone the App asks for App Tracking Transparency permission. If you decline, ads are non-personalised and the App works exactly the same. Change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy choices (EEA/UK) or in your phone's privacy settings. On Android you can reset or delete the advertising ID in the system settings.

Ads are not shown, and no ad is requested, while Unlimited is active. The consent form may still appear once, because your answer also decides whether usage statistics are allowed (section 4).

Learn more: How Google uses data in ads, and the list of providers in the consent form.

4. Usage statistics

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how it is used and to improve it. Measurement starts denied and is switched on only once your consent answer allows it (in the EEA, measurement is a separate purpose from ad personalisation). If you withdraw consent, collection stops in the same session.

  • Events: a lesson started or completed, a puzzle attempted or revealed, a game started or ended (with the AI level and result), a hint used, a rewarded ad offered or watched, the screen opened, the consent outcome.
  • User properties: App language, chosen AI level, piece labels, a range of lessons finished (e.g. “10–24”), consent outcome.
  • Automatic data: an app instance ID, first open, sessions, device model, OS and App version, approximate country, and, if you allowed it, the advertising ID; ad revenue reported by AdMob.

Analytics never receives your moves, saved games, KIF files, name or email.

5. Purchases (Unlimited)

Unlimited Lifetime Access is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their terms. We never receive your card or bank details.

Purchases are validated by RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). The App sends RevenueCat:

  • an App User ID: a random anonymous ID ($RCAnonymousID:…), or, if you signed in, an ID derived from your Apple or Google account (apple:… / google:…). Your email and name are not sent;
  • the store's receipt or purchase token, with product, price, currency, store country and purchase date;
  • basic technical data: platform, OS and App version, and your IP address when connecting.

The App shows your App User ID and order history in Settings → Unlimited → Purchases & receipts. We use it only to find your purchase if you contact us. Learn more: RevenueCat privacy policy.

6. Optional account

An account exists for one thing: carrying Unlimited to another phone, also between iPhone and Android. You sign in with Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In; ShogiSensei has no password of its own and no server of ours stores your account.

  • Apple or Google confirms who you are to the App and shares your account's user ID, and your name and email address (with Apple you can hide your email).
  • The name and email are kept only on your phone, to show who is signed in.
  • The account's user ID becomes your RevenueCat App User ID (section 5), so your purchase is found on any phone where you sign in.
  • Progress, games and settings are not synced.

Sign out or delete the account in Settings → Account. See Delete account for what is removed.

7. Sharing, export and photos

Game records (KIF), daily results and image cards are created on your phone and handed to the system share sheet; you choose where they go, and they never pass through us. Saving an image card to your photos asks for add-only access to your photo library; the App never reads your photos. Importing reads only the text you paste or the file you pick.

8. App updates

The App checks Expo's update service for small updates delivered outside the store. The request contains the App version, platform and update channel, and your IP address reaches the service as with any connection. No personal data is added.

10. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): personalised ads, storing and reading the advertising ID, and usage statistics. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Privacy choices, without affecting earlier processing.
  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): providing Unlimited you bought and the optional account that carries it.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): showing non-personalised ads to fund a free App, preventing ad fraud, delivering updates and answering support requests.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): keeping purchase records as required by tax and accounting law.

11. How long data is kept

  • On your phone: until you clear it in the App or uninstall the App.
  • Usage statistics: no longer than 14 months.
  • Ad data: according to the policies of Google and each ad partner.
  • RevenueCat and store purchase records: as long as needed for the purchase to work, refunds and legal obligations, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Support emails: as long as needed to resolve your request.

12. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to withdraw consent. In the App you can:

  • change ad and analytics consent in Settings → Privacy choices;
  • reset your progress in Settings → Reset progress, and delete games in the Library;
  • export your games as KIF;
  • sign out or delete your account in Settings → Account;
  • ask us to delete your RevenueCat purchase data or anything else: write to dawikk.apps@gmail.com with your App User ID.

We reply within one month. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority; in Poland it is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO, uodo.gov.pl).

13. Children

The App is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of a children's or family programme. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country). If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Security

All connections use TLS. Your progress never leaves your phone, so it is as safe as the phone itself; protect it with a screen lock. Sign-in is handled by Apple and Google; ShogiSensei never sees your password.

15. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when the App gains a feature. The new version is published on this page with a new effective date. The previous version, dated 10 July 2024, is replaced by this one.
