1. The service

ShogiSensei teaches Shogi (Japanese chess) with an interactive course, and lets you play an AI opponent that runs on your device, play another person on one device, solve daily mating problems (tsume), review and analyse games, and import and export game records. The course, the AI at every level, the daily puzzles, game review and the library are free.

The AI, its hints, evaluations and game reviews are produced by a computer engine. They are strong but not perfect, and lessons may contain mistakes; if you find one, please tell us.
