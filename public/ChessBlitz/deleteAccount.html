Delete your account

Last updated: 13 October 2026

An account in Chess Puzzles Blitz exists only if you signed in with Google or Apple. If you always played with Continue without signing in, there is no account and nothing about your games is stored on our server; uninstalling the App removes all its data from your phone.

Delete it in the App

  1. Open Chess Puzzles Blitz and go to Settings.
  2. In the Account section tap your name to open your Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account at the bottom of the screen.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted at once and you are signed out.

Can't use the App?

Email dawikk.apps@gmail.com with the subject “Chess Puzzles Blitz – delete my account”, from the email address of the Google or Apple account you signed in with. If you used Apple's “Hide My Email”, say so and add your display name from the rankings. We will confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: Google or Apple account identifier, email address, name, profile picture, username and display name.
  • Your rating, XP, level, league and totals of games, puzzles and playing time.
  • Your full game history and every puzzle attempt.
  • Your daily streak and daily statistics.
  • Your positions in all rankings.
  • Device integrity records linked to your account.

Deletion from our server is immediate and cannot be undone. If you sign in again later, a new, empty account is created.

What is kept

  • Data on your phone: your local rating, best scores, puzzle history and statistics stay on the device until you uninstall the App or clear its storage.
  • Request logs (IP address, installation ID, time of request) are kept for a limited time for security and troubleshooting, and are then deleted.
  • Ad data collected by Google AdMob and its partners is handled under their own policies. You can reset your advertising ID in the device settings.

Signed in with Apple?

To also remove the App's access to your Apple ID, open Settings → [your name] → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple on your iPhone, choose Chess Puzzles Blitz and tap Stop Using Apple ID. For Google, go to myaccount.google.com/connections.
