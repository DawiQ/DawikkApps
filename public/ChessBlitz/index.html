Help & FAQ

Answers to the most common questions.

Is the app free?

Yes. Chess Puzzles Blitz is free and supported by ads. There are no purchases or subscriptions in the app.

Can I play without ads?

Watch one short video and you get 4 hours without ads. After that, the option comes back after a 4-hour break. Short videos also unlock a hint, an extra life once per Survival run, or a lost streak.

Do I need an account?

No. Tap Continue without signing in and your scores, rating and statistics are kept on your phone. Sign in with Google or Apple to appear in the rankings, keep a daily streak and keep your progress when you change phones.

What happens when I make a mistake?

In the 3- and 5-minute challenges the run goes on and you get the next puzzle, but the clock keeps running. In Survival a mistake costs one of your three lives. In Practice there is no penalty at all.

Where do the puzzles come from?

The puzzles come from the open Lichess puzzle database, which is made of positions from real games played on lichess.org. They are built into the app, so a new puzzle loads instantly.

Which name is shown in the rankings?

After your first sign-in you choose the display name shown in the rankings (2–50 characters). To change the name later, write to us. Names that are offensive or impersonate someone else may be removed.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Go to Settings → Privacy → Ad Preferences to open the consent form again, or Reset Privacy Settings to be asked from scratch. More in the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Account → Profile → Delete Account. Details on the Delete account page.
