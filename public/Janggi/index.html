Janggi in two minutes

Two armies, Cho (楚, green or blue) and Han (漢, red), on a 9×10 board. Pieces stand on the intersections. Checkmate the opposing General to win. Cho moves first.

General궁 · 楚 / 漢

One point along any line of the 3×3 palace, diagonals included. Never leaves the palace.

Guard사 · 士 · 3 points

Moves exactly like the General and also stays in the palace.

Chariot차 · 車 · 13 points

Any distance along a rank or file, like a rook, and along the palace diagonals.

Cannon포 · 包 · 7 points

Moves and captures by jumping over exactly one piece. It can never jump over or capture another cannon.

Horse마 · 馬 · 5 points

One step straight, then one diagonally outward. Blocked if the first point is occupied.

Elephant상 · 象 · 3 points

One step straight, then two diagonally outward. Any piece on the way blocks it.

Soldier졸 / 병 · 卒 / 兵 · 2 points

One point forward or sideways, never back. In the enemy palace it may also step forward along a diagonal.

Choose your setup

Before the game each side swaps its horses and elephants into one of four formations. The app lets you pick yours.

You may pass

Instead of moving you can pass, except when in check. Two passes in a row end the game in a draw.

Bikjang 빅장

Generals facing each other on an open file is not check. If both sides pass during bikjang, the game is decided on points.

Points and deom

Games that end on points compare the material left. Han, who moves second, gets 1.5 extra points (deom), so there is no tie.

Help & FAQ

Answers to the most common questions.

I have never played Janggi. Where do I start?

Open Lessons from the menu and start with the first one. Each lesson takes a minute or two and you make the moves yourself; the next unlocks when you finish. Then play Jiwoo, the Beginner level, and try the Daily Puzzle.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. The AI, lessons, puzzles, review, analysis and your saved games all work without internet. A connection is only used to load ads and for app updates from the store.

Which AI level should I choose?

Beginner (Jiwoo) suits players who have just learned the rules. Each rank on the ladder is cleared by winning a few games against it; Statistics shows your record and rating against every level. When you win most games, move up.

Why can't I pass?

You cannot pass while your General is in check: answer the check first. If your opponent has just passed, passing again ends the game in a draw, or on points during bikjang; the app asks before it does.

Why did the game end “on points”?

A game is decided on points when both sides pass during bikjang, when a position repeats a fourth time, or after 50 moves without a capture. The app warns you before the last two happen. Han gets 1.5 extra points (deom).

Can I play as Han, or choose my formation?

Yes. In Set up a game choose the opponent, the time control, which side you play and your horse–elephant formation. Playing Han turns the board around; the computer picks its own setup at random.

Can I switch between Hanja and Hangul pieces?

Yes. In Settings → Appearance turn Hanja characters on or off. You can also show coordinates, choose green or blue for Cho on the wood set, and switch light or dark mode.

Which languages are available?

English, Korean and Polish. The app follows your device language by default; change it in Settings → Language.

How do I unlock a board or piece theme?

Open Themes and pick one. Every ad you watch for it lends it to you for a week and counts towards keeping it; after a few ads it is yours for good. The gold piece set is earned by reaching a rating of 1600 or winning 50 games against the AI.

How do I keep or move my games to another phone?

Games are stored only on your phone. In Saved games open a game's menu and choose Copy to copy it as text; on the other phone use Import and paste it.

How do I change my ad and privacy choices?

Go to Settings → Data & privacy → Privacy & ads. Declining personalised ads does not limit anything in the app. Read the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

There is no account and nothing is stored on our side. Settings → Data & privacy → Clear app data deletes every saved game, lesson result and statistic; uninstalling the app removes everything.
