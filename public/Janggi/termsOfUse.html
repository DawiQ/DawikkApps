1. The service

Janggi lets you play Janggi (Korean chess) against an AI opponent that runs on your device, play another person on one device, learn the rules with interactive lessons and demonstrations, solve a daily puzzle, review and analyse games, and save, import and export game records. All of it is free.

The AI, its hints, evaluations and game reviews are produced by a computer engine. They are strong but not perfect, and lessons or puzzles may contain mistakes; if you find one, please tell us. Rules follow common Korean practice; the handling of points, repetition and the move limit is described in the App.

3. Rewards and themes

The gold piece set is earned by the in-game achievement described in the App and cannot be obtained with ads.

Rewards and themes have no cash value, are tied to the App's data on your device, cannot be transferred, and are removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it.
