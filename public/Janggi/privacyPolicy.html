Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

In short

  • Janggi has no account and no server. Your games, lessons, puzzles, statistics, rating and settings are stored only on your phone.
  • The game engine runs on your device. Your moves are never sent anywhere.
  • The App shows ads by Google AdMob. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you choose whether ads may be personalised; on iPhone the system asks whether apps may track you. Saying no changes nothing in the App.
  • There are no analytics, no crash reporting services and no in-app purchases.
  • We do not sell personal data.

1. Who we are

Janggi – Korean Chess (the “App”) is an app for learning and playing Janggi, developed by Dawikk Apps (“we”, “us”), who is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact for anything about privacy: dawikk.apps@gmail.com.

2. Data on your phone

The App, including its Fairy-Stockfish engine, runs entirely on your device. What you do in it is saved in the App's private storage and is never sent to us. We have no server that could receive it.

DataWhat it is used for
GamesThe game in progress, finished and imported games, their reviews, to resume, replay and analyse them.
Lessons and puzzlesCompleted lessons, attempts and hints used, solved daily puzzles and your day streak.
StatisticsWins, losses and draws, your record against each AI level, the ladder, your rating and accuracy.
SettingsLanguage, theme, board and piece sets, Hanja labels, coordinates, sound, haptics, AI level.
Rewards and ad limitsThemes unlocked or on loan, the free hint and review allowances, when the last ad was shown, so ads are not shown too often.
Error logIf the App hits an unexpected error, a short technical log is kept on the phone so you can view it or send it to us yourself. It is never sent automatically.

You can delete all of it in Settings → Data & privacy → Clear app data; uninstalling the App deletes it too. Your phone's own backup (Google or iCloud) may include App data according to your device settings.

3. Ads and consent

The App is free and shows ads: a banner on some screens, an occasional interstitial ad between games, and rewarded ads you choose to watch (for a hint, a game review, deeper analysis or a theme). Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). Ad content is limited to a general (PG) rating.

To show and measure ads, and to prevent ad fraud, Google may collect:

  • the advertising ID of your device (Android Advertising ID, or Apple IDFA only if you allow tracking) and the app set ID;
  • the IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • device and OS model, language, network type, the App's name and version;
  • interactions with ads: impressions, clicks, views of rewarded ads.

Your choice. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the App shows Google's consent form (a certified consent management platform under the IAB TCF) before ads are requested. On iPhone the App asks for App Tracking Transparency permission. If you decline, ads are non-personalised (limited ads) and the App works exactly the same. Change your answer at any time in Settings → Data & privacy → Privacy & ads or in your phone's privacy settings. On Android you can reset or delete the advertising ID in the system settings.

If no ad can be loaded, the reward is usually given anyway. Ads are never required to play.

Learn more: How Google uses data in ads and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Clipboard

When you tap Copy or Copy moves, the App puts the game text on your clipboard. When you tap Paste from clipboard while importing a game, it reads the clipboard once. The App does not read the clipboard at any other time.

5. Store and app updates

The App is downloaded and updated through Google Play or the Apple App Store, which process data under their own privacy policies. We receive only aggregated, anonymous statistics from the stores (such as the number of installs and crashes per device model).

6. Legal bases

For users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland:

  • Personalised ads and storing or reading the advertising ID: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and the ePrivacy rules), which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Non-personalised ads, ad frequency limits and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in funding a free App (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Support emails: our legitimate interest in answering you, or steps you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

7. How long data is kept

  • On your phone: until you clear it in the App or uninstall the App.
  • Ad data: according to Google's policies.
  • Support emails: as long as needed to resolve your request.

8. Your rights and deleting data

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold any of your App data, most of these rights are exercised directly in the App:

  • change ad consent in Settings → Data & privacy → Privacy & ads;
  • delete single games in Saved games, or everything in Settings → Data & privacy → Clear app data;
  • export any game as text with Copy;
  • for data held by Google for ads, use My Ad Center and your device's ad settings.

For anything else write to dawikk.apps@gmail.com. We reply within one month. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority; in Poland it is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO, uodo.gov.pl).

9. Children

The App is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of a children's or family programme. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country). If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Your games and progress never leave your phone, so they are as safe as the phone itself; protect it with a screen lock. Connections made by the ad SDK use TLS.

11. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when the App gains a feature that handles data differently. The new version is published on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be announced in the App's release notes.
