1. What the App is

Xiangqi Master is a single-device game of xiangqi (Chinese chess). It offers play against a computer opponent, hot-seat play between two people on one device, mate puzzles, lessons, a post-game review and a record of your games. The App is not an online service: there are no accounts, no servers of ours, no online opponents, no leaderboards, no chat and no way for one player to contact another.

7. About the game content

The engine and the rules

The App implements the rules of xiangqi as commonly played in Asian competition, including the rule that perpetual check loses. Rule sets differ between federations and between clubs; the App's interpretation is not an arbitration and may not match the rules of a particular tournament you are entering.

Ratings and the review

The rating shown in the App is calculated by the App, from games you played against its own engine, and by design it also moves faster during your first ten games. It is a private yardstick for your own progress — not a federation rating, and not comparable with an official one.

The post-game review is an engine's opinion. Engine evaluations are strong but not infallible, particularly in quiet positions and in endgames, and a move labelled a mistake may be perfectly playable in a human game.

Puzzles and lessons

The puzzles were generated and verified automatically — each is checked for a unique fastest mate and replayed through the rules engine — and the lessons' goal moves are verified the same way. If you nonetheless find a position with a second solution or a lesson that misstates a rule, please write to us; that is a bug and we will fix it.
