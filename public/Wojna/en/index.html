WAR

The card game everyone played on rainy days and long train rides, in a new edition: a table like a staff map, paper cards and a “WAR!” stamp on every tie.

Battles and wars

The highest card takes the pile; a tie means war: one card face down, the next face up. Double, triple… Flip by hand or automatically, at four speeds.

The rules you know

Quick War, Classic, Polish 24, Marathon, Three Down, Life and Death – and house rules: cards face down, collecting, a battle limit, jokers, decks of 24 to 52 cards. Plus Beggar-my-neighbour and Camicia.

With friends over Wi‑Fi

One table on the same network – no internet, no accounts. Join from the list, with a code or a QR code. Each phone shows the game in its own language.

Opponents with character

The Count, Zoe, The Professor and others react to won and lost wars. Ranks, badges, statistics and a history with a battle-by-battle replay.

Tactical War

A hand of three cards and a choice of which to play. Opponents at three levels and hints with chances.

For everyone

14 languages, a screen reader that announces the cards and each battle, large buttons, reduced motion. An ad only when you ask for one.
