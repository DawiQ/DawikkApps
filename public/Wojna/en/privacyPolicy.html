War – Classic Card Game

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

In short

War – Classic Card Game (in Polish “Wojna – gra w karty”; the “App”) is made by Dawikk (“we”, the “Provider”). The App has no user account and no server of ours. The game, the characters, Tactical War, the history and the replays run entirely on your phone, also offline, and your games and statistics stay in the device's storage.

Data leaves the phone only in the cases described below: when you buy War Pro (the store and RevenueCat), when you choose to watch a rewarded ad (Google AdMob), when you play with friends over Wi‑Fi, where phones connect directly to each other in the local network, and when you share a history export yourself.

1. Data stored on the device

The App stores data only locally, in its private storage. We never send it anywhere and have no access to it.

You don't have to give any personal data to play. The characters at the table are fictional, and you appear as “You”.

2. Purchases (War Pro)

War Pro is bought through Google Play or the App Store. The store handles the payment – we have no access to card or other payment details.

The purchase status is verified and stored by RevenueCat, Inc. (USA), acting as our processor. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous identifier assigned to the App (not your name, e‑mail or advertising ID);
  • purchase information: product, price and currency, date, subscription or trial status, and the purchase receipt issued by the store;
  • basic technical data: operating system and version, App version, store country and the IP address of the connection.

This data is used only to grant, renew and restore War Pro (also on another device with the same store account) and for aggregated sales statistics. We never share your games with RevenueCat. RevenueCat's policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Promo codes are redeemed by the store itself: on Android the App opens Google Play with the code filled in, on iPhone the App Store's own sheet. The code never reaches us.

3. Rewarded ads

An ad is shown only when you ask for it – for example to play one more game at a large table today, watch one more replay, or try a look for 24 hours. Never during a game and never with War Pro.

Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited). When an ad is shown, Google may process the device's advertising ID, the IP address, the approximate location derived from it, device data, and information about the ad view and your interaction with it.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the App shows Google's consent form before the first ad. Without consent to personalisation, ads are non-personalised.
  • You can change your choice at any time in the App: Settings (⚙︎) → Ad privacy.
  • You can reset or disable the advertising ID in your phone's system settings.
  • On iPhone the App does not ask for tracking permission (ATT) and does not track you across other companies' apps.

How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Playing with friends over Wi‑Fi

Phones at one table connect directly to each other in the local network (Wi‑Fi or a hotspot), without the internet and without a server of ours. They exchange: the name typed at the table, the game settings (rules, deal seed, chosen characters and table look), card flips and Tactical War moves, readiness signals and quick reactions from a fixed list (“Bravo”, “Oops”…). There is no chat.

The phone hosting a table announces it in the local network (table name, free seats, rules) so other phones on the same network can find it. The announcement ends when the table closes. The table code and QR code contain the phone's local network address.

6. History export

The history export (War Pro, in Profile, in the game history) creates a JSON text file on the phone with your games and statistics and hands it to the system share sheet. You decide where it goes. The file contains no personal data other than names typed at Wi‑Fi tables, and it never passes through a server of ours.

7. Third-party services

  • Google Play and the App Store – distribution, payments, promo codes. The stores may collect crash reports and statistics according to your device settings; we see only aggregated, anonymous statistics in their consoles.
  • RevenueCat – War Pro purchase status (section 2).
  • Google AdMob – rewarded ads (section 3).
  • Expo – the framework the App is built with. The production App uses no Expo service that collects data while the App runs.

The App contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools (such as Firebase Analytics or Sentry).

8. Legal bases and transfers

Purchase data is processed because it is necessary to perform the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Personalised ads are shown on the basis of your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), non-personalised ads on the basis of our legitimate interest in funding the free version (Art. 6(1)(f)). RevenueCat and Google may process data outside the EEA, including in the USA, under standard contractual clauses or adequacy decisions (EU‑US Data Privacy Framework).

9. Retention and deletion

  • You can delete the data on the phone in System settings → Apps → War → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the App.
  • RevenueCat keeps purchase information as long as it is needed to provide War Pro and for accounting. To have it deleted, write to us – we will need an identifier, which we will establish together, e.g. from the store's purchase receipt.
  • Advertising data is kept by Google according to its policy.
  • If Android backup or iCloud backup is on, a copy of the App's data may be stored in your Google or Apple account – a system mechanism controlled by your phone's settings.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. Parents or guardians with questions can write to dawikk.apps@gmail.com.

11. Security

The App's data lives in its private directory, protected by the system. Connections to the store, RevenueCat and Google are encrypted. Connections between phones at a Wi‑Fi table stay within your local network – play on networks you trust.

12. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data, to object, and to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier processing). You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority – in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), or the authority of the country where you live. The data on your phone is fully under your control; for purchase data, write to us.

13. Changes to this policy

We announce changes by publishing a new version on this page with a new effective date. If the App were to start sending data in a way not described above, we will describe it here before such a version reaches the store.

This policy is also available in Polish; both versions have the same content.
