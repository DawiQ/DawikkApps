1. What the App is

Fianchetto is a single-device chess training app. It lets you import chess games (from Lichess, Chess.com, links or PGN), review them with chess engines running on your device, read an automatically generated description of the game, analyse positions, play against computer opponents, and train on puzzles generated from your own games.

The App is not an online service: there are no accounts, no servers of ours holding your games, no online opponents, no chat and no way for one user to contact another.

8. About the chess content

Engine evaluations and move grades

Evaluations, move grades (such as “brilliant”, “mistake” or “blunder”), accuracy figures and the Game Score are produced automatically by engines and by the App's own heuristics. They depend on search depth, the engine selected and the device. They are strong but not infallible, and a move labelled a mistake may be perfectly playable in a human game.

Narration

The written story of a game is generated automatically from the engine analysis. It is intended to help you understand the game and may occasionally be imprecise or describe a plan the players did not have in mind.

Ratings and levels

The approximate Elo of the computer opponents, the “my level” estimate and any rating shown in the App are rough guides, not official ratings, and are not comparable with a FIDE or national federation rating.
