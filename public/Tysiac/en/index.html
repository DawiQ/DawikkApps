Tysiąc, the classic card game to 1000 points: bidding, the talon, marriages and bombs. Against the computer at eight levels or with friends at one table – even without internet.

Play the computer

Eight characters: from Tosia, who is just learning the cards, to the Counsel, who works out every deal to the end. 2, 3 or 4 players. The engine thinks on your phone, with no server.

The rules you like

As on kurnik.pl, Polish house rules or Russian/Ukrainian (bidding by 5, the 880 barrel, three zeros). Every rule is a switch. Extras: 555, four aces, blind bidding.

With friends over Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth

2–4 phones at one table, no internet and no accounts. Join from the list, with a code or a QR code. Bots can fill empty seats, and if someone drops out, a bot plays for them until they are back.

Training, history and reviews

Engine hints in training against the computer, the history of every deal with a replay and a review of your decisions. To start: a tutorial with one guided deal.

Deal of the day and profile

The same cards for everyone, every day. Ranks from Hundred to Thousand, statistics and badges – also kept separately for games with friends.

For everyone

Polish, English, Ukrainian and Russian. The game works offline. Labels for screen readers, large touch targets and gender-neutral texts.
