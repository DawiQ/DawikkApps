Tysiąc – Thousand Card Game

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

In short

Tysiąc – Thousand Card Game (in Polish “Tysiąc – gra w karty”; the “App”) is made by Dawikk (“we”, the “Provider”). The App has no user account and no server of ours. Games against the computer, the game engine, training with hints, the deal history with decision reviews, the deal of the day and the profile run entirely on your phone, also offline, and your games and statistics stay in the device's storage.

Data leaves the phone only in the cases described below: when you buy Tysiąc Pro (the store and RevenueCat), when ads are shown in the free version (Google AdMob), and when you play with friends, where phones connect directly to each other over Wi‑Fi or Bluetooth – without the internet.

1. Data stored on the device

The App stores data only locally, in its private storage. We never send it anywhere and have no access to it.

2. Purchases (Tysiąc Pro)

Tysiąc Pro is bought through Google Play or the App Store. The store handles the payment – we have no access to your card or other payment details.

The purchase status is verified and stored by RevenueCat, Inc. (USA), acting as our processor. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous identifier assigned to the App (not your name, e‑mail or advertising ID);
  • purchase information: product, price and currency, date, subscription or trial status, and the purchase receipt issued by the store;
  • basic technical data: operating system and version, App version, store country and the IP address of the connection.

This data is used only to grant, renew and restore Tysiąc Pro (also on another device with the same store account) and for aggregated sales statistics. We do not share the course of your games with RevenueCat. RevenueCat's policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Promo codes are redeemed by the store itself (“Have a code?” on the Tysiąc Pro screen, or Profile → Purchases → Redeem a code): on Android the App opens Google Play with the code filled in, on iPhone it shows the App Store sheet. The code is never sent to us.

3. Ads in the free version

In the free version the App shows two kinds of ads:

  • Rewarded ads – only when you ask for one, e.g. to play a game against a Tysiąc Pro opponent, to get extra hints or a decision review, or to start a “Pro day”.
  • Full-screen (interstitial) ads – before a new game against the computer: every second game, no more often than every 10 minutes, and never in the first 3 days after installation.

Ads never appear during a deal, at a table with friends, in the tutorial or with Tysiąc Pro.

Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited). When an ad is shown, Google may process the device's advertising ID, the IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device data and information about ad impressions and interactions.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the App shows Google's consent form after the first-run screens. Without consent to personalisation, ads are non-personalised.
  • You can change your choice at any time in the App: Settings (⚙︎ on the start screen) → Ad privacy (shown where Google requires it).
  • You can reset or disable the advertising ID in your phone's system settings.
  • On iPhone, ads are always non-personalised: the App does not ask for tracking permission (ATT) and does not track you across other companies' apps.

How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Playing with friends over Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth

Phones at one table (2–4) connect directly to each other – in the local Wi‑Fi network (the same network or a hotspot) or over Bluetooth Low Energy – with no internet, no accounts and no server of ours.

  • Announcing the table. On Wi‑Fi, the host's phone announces the table in the local network (mDNS/Bonjour): the table name “Tysiąc · host's name · table id”, the players and the protocol version. Over Bluetooth, the host announces the table to phones nearby. The announcement disappears when the table is closed.
  • The table code and QR code contain the host phone's local network address and port and the table id.
  • What is exchanged: the names typed at the table, seats, the rules, each player's own view of the game (only their own cards and what is public), moves, ready flags, connection heartbeats and reconnect tokens.
  • After a deal, its full record (all hands) is sent to every player – like turning the cards over on a real table.
  • There is no chat and there are no reactions. Nothing goes through the internet.

6. The game engine and the deal of the day

The computer opponents, the hints in training and the decision reviews use an engine that runs on your phone – we never send deals to any server. The deal of the day is generated on the phone from the date, which is why everyone gets the same cards; your results stay on the phone.

7. Third-party services

  • Google Play and the App Store – distribution of the App, payments, promo codes. The stores may collect crash reports and statistics according to your device settings; we only see aggregated, anonymous statistics in their consoles.
  • RevenueCat – the status of Tysiąc Pro purchases (section 2).
  • Google AdMob – ads (section 3).
  • Expo – the framework the App is built with. The production version uses no Expo service that collects data while the App runs.

The App contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools (e.g. Firebase).

RevenueCat and Google may process data outside the EEA, including in the USA, on the basis of standard contractual clauses or adequacy decisions (EU‑US Data Privacy Framework).

9. Retention and deletion

  • You can delete the data on your phone in System settings → Apps → Tysiąc → Storage → Clear data or by uninstalling the App.
  • RevenueCat keeps purchase information as long as needed to provide Tysiąc Pro and for accounting. To have it deleted, write to us – we will need an identifier, which we will establish together, e.g. from the store's purchase receipt.
  • Advertising data is kept by Google according to its policy.
  • If Android system backup or iCloud backup is turned on, a copy of the App's data may be stored in your Google or Apple account – this is a system feature controlled by your phone's settings.

10. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions, write to dawikk.apps@gmail.com.

11. Security

The App's data lives in its private directory, protected by the system. Connections with the store, RevenueCat and Google are encrypted. Connections between phones at a table happen only in your local network or over Bluetooth nearby – play on networks you trust.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority – in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes UODO). The data on your phone is fully under your control; for purchase data, write to us.
