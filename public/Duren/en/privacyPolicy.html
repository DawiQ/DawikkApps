Privacy Policy

Wersja polska (the Polish version prevails in case of discrepancies)

In short

Durak – card game (in Polish: Dureń; the “App”) is made by Dawikk (“we”, the “Provider”). The App has no user accounts and no server of ours. The game, the computer players, hints and move reviews run entirely on your phone, also offline, and your games and statistics stay in the device's storage.

Data leaves your phone in only three situations, described below: when you buy Durak Premium (the store and RevenueCat), with ads in the free version (Google AdMob), and when you play with friends over Wi‑Fi or Bluetooth, where the phones connect directly to each other.

1. Data stored on your device

The App stores data only locally, in its private storage. We do not send it anywhere and have no access to it.

You do not need to provide any personal data to play. The characters at the table are fictional, and you appear as “You”.

2. Purchases (Durak Premium)

Durak Premium is bought through Google Play or the App Store. The store handles the payment – we have no access to card or other payment details.

The purchase status is verified and stored by RevenueCat, Inc. (USA), acting as our processor. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous identifier assigned to the App (not your name, e‑mail or advertising ID);
  • purchase information: product, price and currency, date, subscription or trial status, and the store's purchase receipt;
  • basic technical data: operating system and version, App version, store country and the connection's IP address.

This data is used only to grant, renew and restore Durak Premium (also on another device with the same store account) and for aggregated sales statistics. We do not share your games with RevenueCat. RevenueCat's policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Promo codes are redeemed by the store itself: on Android the App opens Google Play with the code filled in, on iPhone the App Store's sheet. The code does not reach us.

3. Ads

The free version shows two kinds of ads. Durak Premium has no ads.

  • Rewarded ads – only when you ask for one, e.g. to play a match with the Captain or the Baroness, get an extra move review, more hints, or try a table look for 24 hours.
  • Full-screen ads – at most a few a day and only after a decided match, with a break between them and not in your first days with the App. Never during a round and never at a friends' table.

Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited). When an ad is shown, Google may process the device's advertising ID, the IP address, an approximate location derived from it, device information, and information about the ad's display and your interaction with it.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, before the first ad the App explains the consent form and then shows Google's consent form. Without consent to personalisation, ads are non-personalised.
  • You can change your choice at any time in the App: Settings (⚙︎) → Ad consent.
  • You can reset or turn off the advertising ID in your phone's system settings.
  • On iPhone the App does not ask for tracking permission (ATT) and does not track you across other companies' apps.

How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Playing with friends over Wi‑Fi or Bluetooth

The phones at one table connect directly to each other – on the local Wi‑Fi network (also a phone's hotspot) or over Bluetooth Low Energy – with no internet and no server of ours. They exchange: the name entered at the table, moves, the game state (each phone receives only its own cards and public information) and quick reactions picked from a fixed list (no free text).

The phone that creates a table announces it on the local network and over Bluetooth (the host's name, table code, rules, number of free seats) so that phones nearby can find it. The announcement disappears when the table is closed.

6. Third-party services

  • RevenueCat – Durak Premium purchase status (section 2).
  • Google AdMob – ads (section 3).
  • Expo – the framework the App is built with. The production version uses no Expo service that collects data while the App runs.

The App contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools (e.g. Firebase Analytics, Sentry).

8. Retention and deletion

  • Delete the data on your phone in System settings → Apps → Durak → Storage → Clear data or by uninstalling the App. Statistics can also be reset in the Profile tab.
  • RevenueCat keeps purchase information as long as needed to provide Durak Premium and for accounting. To have it deleted, write to us – we will need an identifier, which we will establish together, e.g. from the store's purchase receipt.
  • Google keeps advertising data according to its policy.
  • If Android's system backup or iCloud is on, a copy of the App's data may be stored in your Google or Apple account – a system mechanism controlled by your phone's settings.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. If you have questions as a parent or guardian, write to dawikk.apps@gmail.com.

10. Security

The App's data is kept in its private directory, protected by the system. Connections to the store, RevenueCat and Google are encrypted. Connections between phones at a table stay on your local network or within Bluetooth range of a few metres – play on networks you trust.

You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Poland: the President of the Personal Data Protection Office, UODO). The data on your phone is fully under your control; for purchase data, write to us.
