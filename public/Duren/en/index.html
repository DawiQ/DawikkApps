Durak

The classic card game of Durak on your phone. Get rid of your cards before the deck runs out – the last player holding cards is the durak, the fool. Against the computer or with friends at one table.

6♠
Q♥
K♦
A♣

Eight characters

From Lucy, holding cards for the first time, to the Baroness, who works through thousands of deals per move. 2 to 6 players, team play, matches to five duraks.

Rules from different countries

Podkidnoy, Perevodnoy with transfers, the Polish kurnik rules or your own: 24, 36 or 52 cards, first attack limit, who may throw in, trump six swap.

Learn and train

A step-by-step tutorial, training with the engine's hints and card counting, the course of every round and a review of your moves.

With friends over Wi‑Fi or Bluetooth

Everyone on their own phone, no internet and no accounts. Phones at one table can even use different languages.

Five languages

English, Ukrainian, Russian, German and Polish – with character names and card letters in each of them.

Works offline

The game, the computer players and the hints run entirely on your phone. The internet is needed only for purchases and ads.
