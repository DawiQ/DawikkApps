TheLine – one line a day

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

In short

TheLine (the “App”) is made by Dawikk (“we”, “the Provider”). The App has no user account, no server of ours, no analytics and no ads. Your lines, moods, photos, tags and settings are stored only on your phone. We have no access to them and never see them.

The App connects to the internet for one purpose only: to check, buy or restore TheLine Plus (the store and RevenueCat). Journal content is never sent.

1. Data stored on your device

Everything below lives in the App's private storage on your phone. We do not send it anywhere.

DataPurpose
JournalsName, emoji, color, kind (“just me” or “for my child”), order and – for a child's journal – the birth date used to show the child's age on each line.
LinesThe entry text (up to 200 characters), date, mood and creation and edit times. The App extracts #tags and @people from the text and builds a local search index.
PhotosA photo attached to a line is copied into the App's storage, resized and saved with a thumbnail. The original in your photo library is not changed.
Time capsules (Plus)Capsule text, journal, creation date and open date.
SettingsActive journal, theme and appearance, language, first day of the week, reminders and their times, app lock, onboarding progress and the last known TheLine Plus status – so Plus also works offline.

Entries about a child are written by you and stay only on your device. You don't have to provide any personal data to use the App.

2. Purchases (TheLine Plus)

TheLine Plus is bought through the App Store or Google Play. The store handles payment – we have no access to your card or other payment details.

The purchase status is verified and stored by RevenueCat, Inc. (USA), acting as our processor. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous identifier assigned to the App (not your name, email or advertising ID);
  • purchase information: product, price and currency, date, subscription or trial status and the store's purchase receipt;
  • basic technical data: operating system and version, App version, store country and the connection's IP address.

This data is used only to grant, renew and restore TheLine Plus (also on another device with the same store account) and for aggregated sales statistics. RevenueCat never receives your journal content, photos or any data from your entries. Transfers to the USA are based on standard contractual clauses. RevenueCat's policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

3. Notifications

Reminders and “On this day” notifications are scheduled locally on your phone – we use no push notifications and no server. By default the “On this day” notification does not show the entry (“You have a memory from 2 years ago”). If you turn on Show content in notifications, part of a line may be visible on the lock screen.

4. App lock

App lock uses Face ID, a fingerprint or your device passcode through the system. The App only learns whether unlocking succeeded – never biometric data. With the lock on, content is hidden in the recent-apps preview; on Android this also means the system blocks screenshots of the App.

5. Export, import and the year book

Exports (JSON backup, password-encrypted backup, text, CSV) and the year book PDF (Plus) are created on your phone and handed to the system share sheet. You decide where they go – files never pass through a server of ours. A JSON backup contains your entries and photos; keep it somewhere safe or use the encrypted backup. We never store the password of an encrypted backup and cannot recover it.

Import reads the file you choose only on your phone.

7. What we don't do

  • We run no accounts and have no server with your data.
  • We use no analytics, ads or tracking tools.
  • We don't sell or share data with third parties (except RevenueCat as described in section 2).
  • We don't use AI to analyse your entries.

8. Retention and deletion

  • You keep data in the App for as long as you like. Settings → Delete all data erases journals, lines, photos and settings. Uninstalling the App removes all of its data from the phone.
  • RevenueCat keeps purchase data as long as needed to manage the subscription and as required by tax law; on request we delete the anonymous identifier linked to the App.
  • If iCloud or Google system backup is on, a copy of the App's data may be stored in your Apple or Google account – this is a system feature controlled in your phone's settings.

9. Children

The App is meant for adults – including parents keeping a journal about their child. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. Information about a child entered by a parent stays on the parent's device.

10. Security

The App's data lives in its private directory, protected by the system and device encryption. You can additionally turn on the app lock. Connections to the store and RevenueCat are encrypted.

11. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object to the processing of your data, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Poland: the President of the Personal Data Protection Office, UODO). Journal data is fully under your control on the phone; for purchase data, write to us.

12. Changes to this policy

We announce changes by publishing a new version on this page with a new effective date. If the App were to start sending data in any way other than described above, we will describe it here before such a version reaches the store.
