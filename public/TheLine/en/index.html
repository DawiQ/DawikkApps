TheLine

One line a day. Every day, for years. And then – the same day from one, two and five years ago.

2023Rain all day. Pumpkin soup.
2024She rolled over on her own for the first time.
2025Last warm evening on the balcony.
TodayFrost on the car, first beanie of the year.

Twenty seconds

One sentence, up to 200 characters. It saves itself. No streaks, no guilt – you can always write a missed day later.

On this day

Below today's line wait the same days from previous years. Over time, the best part of the app.

A child's journal

A separate journal about your child, with their age on every line: “1 year 5 months”, “2 years 3 months”.

Calendar and mood

Dots for days with a line, a mood view, the whole year on one screen and a yearly review.

#tags and @people

Fast search by words, tags and people – accents optional.

Yours only

No account and no server. Everything stays on your phone, with Face ID lock and full export at any time.

TheLine Plus optional

Writing, your full history, On this day, search, the yearly review and export are and will stay free. Plus adds: up to 10 journals, paper styles and fonts, a year book PDF, multi-year insights and time capsules – lines to your future self. A yearly subscription with 7 days free, or a one-time purchase.
